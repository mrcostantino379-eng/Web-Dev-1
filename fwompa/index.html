Fwompa was caught in Vermilion City at level 27 and almost immediately became a staple member of my team. Fwompa was easily the MVP of this run, with her bulk and typing making her an incredible member of the team. Her amazing special bulk and access to recovery let her take hits like a champ. Will-O-Wisp let her not only take more hits from physical attackers, but let other team members switch in on otherwise lethal hits. Access to the move Recover and the ability Water Absorb lets her hang around a fight far longer than almost any other Pokemon. Hydro pump and Hex are incredible STAB moves, especially with her ability to spread burn increasing the power of Hex by x2. Rain Dance and Protect were only needed in niece scenarios, but when they were needed provided invaluable support. Protect for scouting and stalling the opponent, and Rain Dance for stopping the opponent from using the weather to their advantage. Being able to take a hit and set up Rain Dance sometimes dismantled entire teams. Overall she is an easy five stars and a no brainer to bring to the Elite Four.
