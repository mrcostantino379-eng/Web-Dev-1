Big Fista was hatched from an egg in Cerulean City. Big Fista with Iron Fist and the punching glove item hits really hard. Overall good speed, good attack, and a devastating attacking type with Fire and Fighting makes him hit like a truck. His bulk isn't anything great but with Drain Punch he can heal off a good amount of the damage he takes. U-Turn and Knock Off is also amazing utility. He stayed on my team for most every battle. Him and Fwompa were a powerful duo.
